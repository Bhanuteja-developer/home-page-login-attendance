Why FaceNet / ArcFace Failed You

FaceNet and ArcFace are Face Recognition models. They map facial geometry (eyes, nose, mouth) to spatial vectors to verify identity.

The Exploit: When your employee's friend points Phone A at Phone B's screen, the facial layout is still identical. FaceNet/ArcFace will verify the face 100% because they are mathematically blind to depth, screens, and liveness.

The Solution: Active Liveness Detection

By prompting the web-app to verify physical changes (blinking, turning of the head) in real-time, we ensure a static digital photograph or pre-recorded video cannot bypass your system.
